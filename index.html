Is SUDYP PDF really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, registrations, advertisements, credits or paid downloads.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Files you select are processed directly in your browser. Processing libraries may load from a content delivery network, but documents are not sent there.

Will PDF to Word keep exactly the same layout?

No. The converter creates an editable DOCX from selectable text; images, complex tables, fonts and exact spacing may not carry across.

Can I use it offline?

The site shell may work offline after a visit. PDF processing needs the supporting libraries to be available, so the first use of a tool requires internet access.

Can you compress any PDF?

Scanned pages often benefit. Compression uses page images and can make some documents larger; it also removes selectable text and form interactivity.
