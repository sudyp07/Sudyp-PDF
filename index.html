GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do you upload my PDFs to a server?

No. The PDF document data is processed in your browser. Processing libraries may be downloaded from CDNs if local copies are not yet available.

Does the app work offline?

The website is installable and the app shell works offline. PDF engines work offline after the optional one-time library setup, which GitHub Pages deployment can bundle automatically.

Will compressing always make a PDF smaller?

No. Results depend on the contents. Compression may flatten pages to images and make searchable text unavailable. Actual before-and-after sizes are shown.
