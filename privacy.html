SUDYP PDF

Privacy and local processing

Your documents stay on your device. SUDYP PDF processes supported files directly in your browser without uploading them to our servers.

Files and data

SUDYP PDF does not send your selected documents to an external processing service, and it does not need a backend or database. Files are held in browser memory while you work. Reset the workspace or close the tab to release them. Browser-generated downloads are saved wherever your browser normally downloads files.

Third-party code

Some PDF operations load version-pinned JavaScript libraries from jsDelivr or Cloudflare CDN. Those networks may see ordinary website requests for the JavaScript files (for example, your IP address), but SUDYP PDF does not submit the contents of your documents to them. The ZIP library is shipped with this website.

Preferences

Theme, favorite tools, recently used tool names and manually created flashcards are saved in localStorage on your device. PDF files, file contents, passwords and signatures are not stored in localStorage.

Security limitations

This is not an encryption or malware-scanning service. Do not assume that metadata cleaning removes hidden or embedded information. Do not assume password protection can be removed without authorization. Sensitive or regulated documents may require specialized, independently audited software.

Analytics and ads

No ad network, analytics tracking, account system, or payment service is included in this project.
